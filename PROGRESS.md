The trainer applies `attention_mask` and its original reduction. Accuracy separately makes a contiguous shifted-logit copy, runs full-vocabulary `argmax`, then checks supervised labels. Soup rewind separately runs per-row detached CE and transfers row totals/counts to Python. Their GPU time and CPU synchronization costs have not been measured separately.
- Added `solution/entropy.py`: two original stable tiled reductions compute only per-token entropy, leaving trainer masks, aggregation, accuracy, rewind, logits, and gradients untouched. The hook is limited to the imported TRL SFT entropy helper, version 0.29.1, contiguous fp32 CUDA `[B,T,151936]`, default chunk size, and disabled grad. Other inputs use the original helper. GEMM offers a larger measured category but replacing library matrix multiplication or the LM head has much greater gradient/performance risk; broad cast or elementwise patches lack safe attribution. Entropy is the narrow current experiment, **not a 5% claim**.
- Local numerical tests cover ragged vocabulary tiles, 151,936 classes, large offsets, uniform logits, output dtype/shape, fallback and differentiable calls.
